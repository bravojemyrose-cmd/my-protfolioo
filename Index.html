Stories I adore

My Favorite Manhwa

Beautiful stories, unforgettable characters, and worlds I love.

Action/Adventure/Fantasy/Webtoons/Shounen

Eleceed

My ultimate favorite and comfort manhwa. I love its lovable characters, funny moments, and the heartwarming bond between Jiwoo and Kayden. No matter how many times I read it, it always feels like coming home.

Romance/Webtoon

Remarried Empress

A captivating story of royalty, love, betrayal, and second chances. I admire Navier's intelligence, elegance, and strength as she finds her own happiness and refuses to let others define her worth.

Webtoons/Shoujo/Fantasy/Historical/Drama

Who Made Me A Princess?

A beautiful story filled with magic, royal life, and complicated family bonds. I love its dreamy atmosphere and the emotional journey of Athanasia and her father. It's a story that makes me appreciate the little moments of love, warmth, and belonging.
